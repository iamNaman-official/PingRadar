How a check cycle works

Three steps, repeating on a schedule, entirely in the background.

01 — SCAN

Every site, at once

All tracked URLs are checked concurrently, not one after another — so the total time is bounded by the slowest single response, not the sum of all of them.

02 — DETECT

Real status, not noise

Response time, status code, and up/down are logged every cycle. Rate-limited responses are recognized and skipped, instead of logged as a false outage.

03 — RECORD

History that adds up

Every check is its own row — uptime percentage and the response time chart are just queries over that history, not a separately maintained number.
